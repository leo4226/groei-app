# Floreren

A plant care and garden management app for households. Users register plants, log care tasks, and visualise their outdoor gardens and indoor spaces on Maps.

## Language

**Map**:
The visual representation of a physical space (outdoor or indoor) on which Plants and Objects are placed.
_Avoid_: Canvas (implementation detail), Garden (too narrow — a Map can also be a balcony or rooftop), Floor plan

**Map type**:
Either `outdoor` (a garden, balcony, or any open-air space — has GPS location and sun simulation) or `indoor` (a floor plan with rooms and walls — no sun features).
_Avoid_: "garden map", "house map"

**Plant**:
A specific plant owned by the household — a named individual with its own care schedule, location, and history.
_Avoid_: Plant record, Plant instance

**Species**:
A botanical plant type (e.g. Monstera deliciosa) that provides shared care knowledge — watering frequency, sun needs, frost sensitivity — fetched from external sources and stored locally.
_Avoid_: Plant type, Plant info, Variety (too specific)

**Zone**:
A named area on a Map with a surface type (soil, deck, lawn, gravel, etc.) drawn as a polygon. Used for sun analysis and plant suitability.
_Avoid_: Ground zone (implementation name), Area, Region

**Container**:
An Object placed on a Map that can hold one or more Plants (e.g. pot, planter, raised bed).
_Avoid_: Object (too generic)

**Hardscape**:
An Object placed on a Map that is not a plant holder (e.g. bench, table, stepping stone, rain barrel).
_Avoid_: Object (too generic), Furniture (too narrow)

**Placing**:
The act of assigning a Plant or Object a position on a Map (setting its SVG coordinates). A Plant can exist in the registry without being placed.
_Avoid_: Adding, pinning, dropping

**Care schedule**:
A recurring rule on a Plant defining how often a care action should happen (e.g. water every 7 days). Default interval is inherited from the Species when a Plant is first added, and can be overridden per Plant.
_Avoid_: Reminder, Interval

**Care task**:
A due or overdue care action for a specific Plant, surfaced on the dashboard. Generated from a Care schedule. Triggered by time.
_Avoid_: Reminder, Todo, Alert

**Care log**:
An immutable record that a Care task was completed (or skipped), by whom, and when.
_Avoid_: History entry, Event

**Alert**:
An environmental or condition-based warning for a Plant — triggered by weather thresholds (drought, waterlogging, cold stress, heat stress, bring-inside temperature) or by a fertilise window opening. Distinct from a Care task: Alerts are triggered by external conditions, not by a schedule. For `indoor` Maps, drought and waterlogging Alerts do not apply (the plant is not exposed to rain). `bring_inside` Alerts never apply to Plants on indoor Maps.
_Avoid_: Care task (schedule-based), Notification (UI concept)

**Care thresholds**:
Environmental limits stored per Plant (and cached on its Species) defining when conditions become stressful — e.g. drought below 5mm/week, cold stress below 5°C. Used to compute Alerts. Generated by Claude Haiku when a Plant is added and cached on the Species for reuse.
_Avoid_: Settings, Preferences, Limits

**Top alert**:
The single highest-severity Alert for a Plant, embedded in the Map view payload for efficient display. Drives the halo colour and badge icon on a Plant marker without requiring a per-plant API call. Full Alert details are fetched lazily when a Plant is tapped.
_Avoid_: Alert summary (ambiguous with the `/alerts/summary` endpoint)

**Household**:
A group of one or more Accounts that share Maps, Plants, and care data. Every piece of user data (Maps, Plants, Care logs) belongs to a Household, not to an individual Account. Created automatically when the first member registers.
_Avoid_: User group, Team, Family

**Account**:
A registered user with email and hashed password credentials. Has a display name and belongs to exactly one Household. The login identity — distinct from the old User concept (which was a simple household toggle, now replaced).
_Avoid_: User (ambiguous with the old toggle-based User), Profile

**Household member**:
The relationship between an Account and a Household. An Account is a member of exactly one Household.
_Avoid_: User, Participant

**Session**:
A JWT token issued on login, stored in localStorage, and sent with every API request. Identifies the Account and their Household for all data access. Expires after a fixed duration (to be configured).
_Avoid_: Cookie, Auth token (too generic)

## Relationships

- A **Household** owns zero or more **Maps**, **Plants**, and **Care logs**
- An **Account** belongs to exactly one **Household**
- A **Map** is either `outdoor` (has GPS, sun simulation) or `indoor` (floor plan, no sun)
- A **Map** contains zero or more **Zones**, **Containers**, **Hardscapes**, and **Plants**
- A **Plant** can exist without being Placed on a Map (registry-only)
- A **Plant** can be Placed directly on a Map, or inside a **Container** on a Map
- A **Species** provides care knowledge for a **Plant** — one Species, many Plants. Stores the default Care schedule interval (e.g. water every 10 days) and Care thresholds.
- A **Care schedule** generates **Care tasks** shown on the dashboard
- Completing a **Care task** produces a **Care log** entry

## Example dialogue

> **Dev:** "When Leon places a Monstera on the Garden Map, does it go directly on the Map or inside a Container?"
> **Domain expert:** "Either. If it's a floor plant it goes directly on the Map. If it's in a pot, you Place the Container on the Map and then assign the Plant to that Container."

> **Dev:** "Is a balcony an outdoor Map or an indoor Map?"
> **Domain expert:** "Outdoor — it gets sun simulation. Indoor is only for fully enclosed floor plans."

**Watchlist**:
A personal flag on a Plant indicating the household wants elevated care visibility for it — e.g. a prized avocado tree or homegrown peppers. Does not change the care schedule or thresholds; only affects how prominently the Plant is surfaced in the care UI.
_Avoid_: Priority (implies ranking), Favourite (implies affection not care intent), Alert level

**Icon**:
A named SVG file in the icon manifest (`icons/manifest.json`) providing the visual identity of a Species on the map and in the picker. Every Icon has a canonical base entry; additional entries for the same Species are Variants.
_Avoid_: Plant image, Sprite

**Icon variant**:
An alternative Icon for the same Species, distinguished by `form` and/or `phase`. Linked to its base entry via `variant_of`. File naming: `{base}_{form}_{phase}.svg` (e.g. `tomato_bare_seedling.svg`). The base Icon is shown when no matching variant exists.
_Avoid_: Sub-icon, Alternative icon

**Form** (icon dimension):
How a plant is visually presented in its Icon: `potted` (shown in a pot), `bare` (no pot, in-ground), `portrait`, or `fruit`. Automatically selected from placement context — a Plant placed inside a Zone of type "plant bed" uses the `bare` form. Additional auto-selection rules may be added later.
_Avoid_: Style, Type (overloaded)

**Phase** (icon dimension):
The life stage encoded in an Icon variant: `seed`, `seedling`, or `mature`. Stored as a field on the Plant (defaults to `mature` so existing plants are unaffected). Selected manually by the household member; automatic suggestion from plant age or phenology is a future option.
_Avoid_: Stage, Growth stage, Age

**Icon request**:
An explicit signal from a household member that a Plant needs a custom Icon created. Stored as `icon_requested = true` on the Plant. Distinct from a Plant that simply has no `icon_key` — a Plant can have no icon without an active request. Surfaces in the Settings icon gap view for Leon to act on.
_Avoid_: Icon order, Icon ticket

## Flagged ambiguities

- "Object" was used in code to mean both Container and Hardscape — resolved: use Container or Hardscape. "Object" is an internal DB category only.
- "Garden" was used as a Map type — resolved: use `outdoor`. A balcony is outdoor, not a garden.
- "Ground zone" was an implementation name for Zone — resolved: use Zone.
